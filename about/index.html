What is Pawnyable?

Pawnyable is an intermediate website for learning Binary Exploitation (pwnable).
I created this site because there was no site where intermediate to advanced users could learn Pwn technology in Japanese.

Target audience

This course is aimed at people who have basic skills such as reading and writing assembly, knowledge of BOF and UAF, and ROP construction.
